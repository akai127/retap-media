Straight talk, per Florida law: homeowners are responsible for payment of their insurance deductible. Anyone who offers to waive it, rebate it, or "eat it" is committing insurance fraud — and handing you a fraud problem too. (Fla. Stat. §489.147)

Watch for storm chasers. After every named storm, out-of-town crews knock doors promising "free roofs." No FL license you can look up, no local address, gone by the time the warranty matters. Look up any roofer — including us — at MyFloridaLicense.com before you sign anything.

FAQ

Will filing a claim raise my rates?
That's between you and your carrier, and we won't pretend to know your policy — we're roofers, not your insurance advisor. What we can do is document the damage thoroughly and free, so the decision you and your carrier make is based on the real condition of the roof.
What does the inspection cost?
Nothing. You get the photo file either way, even if the answer is "your roof is fine." About a third of our inspections end exactly that way.
Can you waive my deductible?
No — and nobody legally can. Under Florida law the deductible is yours to pay, and a contractor offering to absorb it is committing insurance fraud with your name on the paperwork. We'd rather tell you that on a website than have you learn it from an adjuster.
How fast can you get to me after a storm?
Our target is an inspection within 48 hours inside Brevard. Call or text (321) 977-0308 — the phone is answered by a Cox, and every message gets a response in under a minute.
