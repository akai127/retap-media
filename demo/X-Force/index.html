Questions we get every week

FAQ

Do you take small jobs?
Yes. A dead outlet, a ceiling fan, a breaker that won't hold — those are most of what we do. Small jobs get quoted on the phone so you know the number before anyone drives over.
What does an estimate cost?
Nothing. Phone quotes for small work, a free walk-through for anything bigger. The number you get is the number you pay unless we find something and tell you first.
Do you pull permits?
Yes, wherever the city or county requires one — panel changes, service upgrades, new circuits, and remodel wiring. The inspection is part of the job, not an extra.
Do you do commercial work?
Yes. Small commercial build-outs, tenant improvements, lighting, and dedicated equipment circuits. Call and describe the space — we'll tell you straight if it's a fit.
How fast can you get to me?
Call or text (501) 749-3121 — every message gets a response in under a minute, and you'll get a real arrival window, not a "we'll call you back." No-power situations get moved to the front of the line.
