Questions we get every week

FAQ

Should I repair or replace?
Depends on the age, the part, and the repair history — and we'll give you both numbers so you can decide. Rough rule: a unit under ten years old with a single failed part gets fixed. A fifteen-year-old unit with a dead compressor doesn't.
What does a service call cost?
You'll get the diagnostic price when you book, before anyone drives over, and the repair quote before any work starts. No surprise line items.
Do you work on ductwork under the house?
Yes — it's a big part of what we do. Repairs, re-routes for plumbing or foundation work, sealing, and replacing runs that have come apart. If your system runs fine but the back bedroom never cools, that's usually ducts, not the unit.
Do you install new systems?
Yes. Sized to the house, not to the sale. You get a written quote, the install is tested with you there, and Wayne is the one who answers if anything's off afterward.
How fast can you get to me?
Call or text (501) 615-4923 — every message gets a response in under a minute and a real arrival window. No-cooling calls in the summer and no-heat calls in the winter go to the front of the line.
