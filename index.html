Questions, answered straight

What owners ask us first.

What does Retap Media do?

We build appointment systems for home-services companies. The core is database reactivation — compliant text and email campaigns that go back through the leads you already paid for and turn them into booked, qualified appointments — plus under-60-second follow-up on new leads, an AI receptionist, booking funnels, and conversion websites.

What is database reactivation?

Every business that buys leads has a CRM full of contacts that got one call and went quiet. Reactivation is a structured campaign that re-opens those conversations — messages that read like a person wrote them, sent from numbers registered to your business — and works every reply to a booked appointment or a clean no. The leads are already paid for, so recovered deals carry near-zero acquisition cost.

Do I need to buy new leads or increase ad spend?

No. The system points at the lead list you already own. Zero new ad spend is the core of the offer — we work with businesses that have 500+ leads sitting in their database.

Is the texting compliant?

Compliance is built in: numbers carrier-registered to your business, first-party contacts only, opt-outs honored instantly in any wording, business-hours sending, and a full consent and message log that you own.

How fast can we launch?

Roughly two weeks from signing: we connect your CRM and lead sources, register your texting numbers with the carriers, clean the list, and get your sign-off on every script. Reactivated appointments typically start booking within the first week of messages going live.

Which industries do you work with?

Residential solar is live today through the Solar Appointment Engine. Pools & spas open next; generator dealers and HVAC are in the build queue. If your business buys leads, runs a CRM, and closes in-home or over the phone, the engine fits.

Do you replace my CRM or my sales team?

No. Everything plugs into the CRM and sales process you already run. Your closers keep closing — the system fills their calendar with qualified, confirmed appointments and routes ambiguous replies to a human.
