How it works

Live in about two weeks, on a launch date we confirm together.

Signing day starts onboarding; it doesn't switch the system on. Here's what the two weeks actually contain.

Days 1–3 · Kickoff

Credentials, requirements, numbers

We collect CRM and website access, agree the qualification questions and hours, and file your texting numbers for carrier registration under your business name on day one — because carrier approval is the long pole and nobody controls its clock.

Days 4–10 · Build & approve

Wired, scripted, tested

Forms, call routing, booking, and pipelines get connected to your CRM. You approve every script word before anything sends. We run test inquiries through the whole path and show you the timestamps.

≈ Day 14 · Launch confirmed

Email and calls live; texting when carriers clear

On the confirmed launch date, form response, call routing, and booking go live. Texting activates the moment carrier registration is approved — usually within the same window, sometimes later — and we tell you the day it happens rather than promising it in advance.

How we price and behave

Outcomes over activity. Always.

✓We count sits, not "bookings"

Response time, qualification, times offered, appointments booked, appointments sat, and revenue are six different numbers. We report all six separately, and an appointment only counts when a qualified decision-maker actually sits — reconfirmed, transcript attached. No-shows get replaced, not tallied.

✓Guarantees in writing

Every engagement is built around a written outcome number. Miss it, and the bill stops until we've hit it.

✓Compliant by design

Questions, answered straight

What owners ask us first.

What does Retap Media do?

We build the systems that help your business respond, book, and follow up: websites, forms and CRM integration, booking, AI answering, database reactivation, and follow-up automation. The core is speed to lead — every website form fill and missed call answered conversationally in under 60 seconds, nights and weekends included, qualified with your questions, and offered real times on your calendar. You can take the integrated system or a single focused build. We don't sell lead generation; we improve the demand pathways you already have.

Can I hire you for just one thing?

Yes. Most clients start with the integrated system, but a website, a forms-to-CRM integration, a booking flow, AI answering, or a reactivation campaign can each be scoped on its own. Tell us what you need and we'll answer about that service — you won't be pushed into a generic demo.

What is database reactivation?

Every business that buys leads has a CRM full of contacts that got one call and went quiet. Reactivation is a structured campaign that re-opens those conversations — messages that read like a person wrote them, sent from numbers registered to your business — and works every reply to a booked appointment or a clean no. The leads are already paid for, so recovered deals carry near-zero acquisition cost.

Do I need to buy new leads or increase ad spend?

No. The system works the inquiries you already generate and the leads you already own. Zero new ad spend — the point is to stop losing the demand you're already paying for.

Is the texting compliant?

Compliance is built in: numbers carrier-registered to your business, first-party contacts only, opt-outs honored instantly in any wording, business-hours sending, and a full consent and message log that you own.

How fast can we launch?

Onboarding takes about two weeks: credentials and requirements, numbers and integrations, your approval of every script, testing, and a launch date we confirm together. Email response and call routing go live on that date. Texting activates when the carriers approve your business registration — we file it on day one, but we can't guarantee their timing.

Which industries do you work with?

Our appointment playbooks are built for home services: residential solar is live today through the Solar Appointment Engine, pools & spas open next, and generator dealers and HVAC are in the build queue. Websites, CRM integration, booking, and AI answering aren't trade-specific, so any business that takes inquiries can hire us for those. Tell us what you need and we'll say honestly whether we're a fit.

Do you replace my CRM or my sales team?

No. Everything plugs into the CRM and sales process you already run. Your closers keep closing — the system answers, qualifies, offers times, confirms, and routes ambiguous replies to a human.

I'm already a client. How do I get support?

Email preston@retapmedia.com or use the client support page. Every request goes to a named owner, and you get a human response within one business day — usually much faster during staffed hours.
