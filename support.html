Something's off with your system? Tell us.

This page is for current Retap Media clients. Submit the form below and it goes straight to a named owner. Our target is a human response in under 24 hours, including weekends and holidays. New to Retap? Start here instead.

How this works

Tell us what's happening and include a screenshot if helpful — the form accepts one image, and it stays private to our team. A client ID helps but is never required.

Our team will review your request and follow up by email. We aim to respond within 24 hours, including weekends and holidays.

Calls or booking down?

If inquiries aren't being answered or appointments aren't booking, that's critical. Say so in the issue description, or call (321) 215-7267 and say it's a live-system outage. Both founders are alerted immediately.

! Please don't include passwords, API keys, or login credentials in the description or in screenshots. If we need access, we'll arrange it through a secure channel when we follow up.

Prefer email? Write to preston@retapmedia.com with your company name and what's happening. The form above is the fastest route to the support owner, but either way a human follows up.
