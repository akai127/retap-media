This page is for current Retap Media clients. Every request goes to a named owner with a response target — not a message that lands in someone's inbox and waits. New to Retap? Start here instead.

How to reach us

Email preston@retapmedia.com or use the form below, which drafts that email for you. Include your company name; a client ID helps but is never required.

Response target: a human reply within four business hours when we're staffed, and always within one business day.

Calls or booking down?

If inquiries aren't being answered or appointments aren't booking, that's critical. Mark it that way in the form, or call (321) 215-7267 and say it's a live-system outage. Both founders are alerted immediately.

How we work a request:
  1. New — received and logged.
  2. Assigned — a named owner is on it (Patrick, with Preston as backup).
  3. In progress — being worked; you get an update at least daily until resolved.
  4. Waiting on client — we need something from you; the ticket says exactly what.
  5. Resolved — fixed and confirmed with you. Reopen any time by replying to the thread.
